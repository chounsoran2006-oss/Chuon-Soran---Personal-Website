📚 All Lessons (8 Modules)

MODULE 1 Foundations

Parts of Speech & Basic Sentence Structure

Master nouns, pronouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, prepositions, and build simple (Subject + Verb + Object) sentence patterns.

MODULE 2 Tenses Focus

Mastering Present, Past & Future Simple/Continuous Tenses

Learn time markers, state vs action verbs, and how to express daily routines, completed actions, and ongoing activities clearly.

MODULE 3 Advanced Tenses

Perfect & Perfect Continuous Tenses Mastery

Understand Present Perfect vs Simple Past differences, Past Perfect sequencing, and duration markers (for/since/already).

MODULE 4 Verbs & Modals

Modal Verbs, Auxiliary Verbs & Subject-Verb Agreement

Express ability, obligation, permission, and probability accurately while avoiding agreement errors with singular/plural subjects.

MODULE 5 Passive & Causative

Active vs Passive Voice & Causative Structures

Transform active sentences into passive for formal and academic writing, and master causative forms (have/get something done).

MODULE 6 Complex Sentences

Relative Clauses, Compound & Complex Sentences

Connect ideas using conjunctions (FANBOYS), defining/non-defining relative clauses (who, which, that), and subordinating conjunctions.
